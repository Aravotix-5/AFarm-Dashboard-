About A Farm

A Farm is a family-owned backyard farm in Virginia. Our half-acre backyard holds roughly a quarter acre of growing space, started about a year to a year and a half ago. We water with rainwater whenever we can, keep a hose on hand for the rest, and run our setup on a solar-chargeable power bank. Today we grow 70+ crop varieties.
